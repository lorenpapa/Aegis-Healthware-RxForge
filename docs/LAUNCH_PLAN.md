## Current RxForge prototype

RxForge is an interactive outpatient-pharmacy training prototype. It uses only
fictional people and scenarios and does not collect or store patient or learner
information.

The current release includes:

- A polished product landing page
- 15 decision-based pharmacy scenarios
- Patient identification and wrong-patient mismatch recognition
- Product-selection and unclear-prescription safeguards
- Counseling and clinical-question handoff judgment
- Privacy and authorization judgment
- Controlled-substance escalation and inventory concerns
- Medication disposal, storage, labeling, safety reporting, and near-miss topics
- Immediate contextual feedback with FDA, DEA, or HHS references
- A session score, completion summary, and restart flow
- A passing score of 12 out of 15 decisions (80%)
- Responsive mobile and desktop layouts

The module currently runs entirely in browser memory. There is no login,
database-backed progress, learner account, certificate, or analytics collection.

Training content is educational. Technician responsibilities vary by
jurisdiction and must follow applicable law, pharmacist supervision, and
pharmacy policy. Scenario content and its FDA, DEA, and HHS references still
require review by a licensed pharmacist familiar with the target jurisdiction
before launch.

## Evidence to build next

1. Have a licensed pharmacist familiar with the target jurisdiction review all
   scenarios, explanations, scoring language, and FDA, DEA, and HHS source links.
2. Recruit 5–10 pharmacy trainees or coworkers as voluntary testers.
3. Record only non-sensitive feedback: completion rate, average score, confusing
   questions, and feature requests.
4. Validate the implemented 12/15 (80%) pass threshold with reviewers and pilot
   users.
5. Add automated tests for question selection, scoring, progression, completion,
   and restart behavior.
6. Publish a short case study describing the problem, design decisions,
   implementation, and measured results.
7. Add repository screenshots and a concise product architecture overview.
8. Consider future modules only after the first module has been reviewed and
   tested with real learners.
9. Create the Aegis Healthware LinkedIn company page after the public demo and
   repository are ready.
10. Add the role to Loren Papa's LinkedIn and résumé using the actual project
    start date.

## Pre-launch accuracy checklist

- Confirm every pharmacy statement with a qualified reviewer.
- Confirm that each reference link directly supports its scenario.
- Confirm that the clearly stated passing score remains 12/15 (80%).
- Test keyboard, screen-reader, mobile, and desktop behavior.
- Confirm the public URL, privacy wording, and educational disclaimer.
- Complete a formal name and trademark search before commercial registration.
